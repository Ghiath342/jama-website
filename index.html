Prefabricated Building System

Inside a JAMA Home

Every JAMA villa stands on a prefabricated frame made from hot-dip galvanised steel, cold-formed into precise sections. Together with insulated walls, a light roof and engineered floors, it replaces traditional construction with a faster, cleaner and stronger way to build.

LightweightAbout one-fifth the weight of a brick-and-concrete building.
Faster to buildFactory-made parts that are quick to transport and assemble.
Wind & quake resistantA rigid steel frame that stays stable under strong winds and tremors.
Insulated & quietRock wool and insulated panels keep the home comfortable and quiet.
Easy to extendRooms and floors can be added later.
GreenerDry construction with recyclable steel and less waste on site.

A two-storey family villa on a 13.74 × 10 m footprint — open living downstairs, private bedrooms upstairs.

Galvanised steel studs, joists and roof trusses are cut and pre-assembled in the factory, then fixed together on site with self-drilling screws and steel connectors.

  1. Roof systemSteel roof trusses, OSB structural board, a waterproof layer and light roof tiles — usually asphalt shingles.
  2. Floor systemCold-formed steel joists or composite beams with OSB floor boards, supports and connectors.
  3. Wall systemWall studs, top and bottom tracks, bracing and wall panels, joined with steel connectors.
  4. Insulation systemRock wool or glass wool in the walls and roof, plus insulated outer panels, for lasting thermal and sound comfort.

The insulated outer wall panel

1Metal face

Galvalume-coated steel (55% aluminium) with an embossed, colour-fast finish.

2Insulating core

High-density, flame-retardant polyurethane foam that blocks heat, fire and moisture.

3Foil backing

Aluminium foil that stops water vapour and reflects heat back into the home.

How It Works

From First Idea to Final Handover

A clear, structured process that keeps you informed at every step.

1

Initial Consultation

We learn about your intended use, location, land, requirements, style and approximate budget.

2

Model Selection

Choose the JAMA model that suits you from our catalogue.

3

Technical Review

Project requirements, site conditions, specifications and options are reviewed.

4

Proposal & Agreement

Receive a clear proposal covering the selected scope, specifications and next steps.

5

Production

The approved building components are prepared according to the final specifications.
